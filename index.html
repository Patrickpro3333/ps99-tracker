How to read the stats
  • Rank is your place on the leaderboard. #1 is the best.
  • Points per hour is how fast points are coming in. It comes from snapshots the site saves every hour, plus one every minute while this page is open.
  • Gains are points added in the last 1, 6, 12, and 24 hours, from the same snapshots. The site keeps snapshots for the top 2,000 teams only, so gains for other teams fill in while this page is open. Clan points restart when a new clan war begins, so clan gains, rank change and the chart count from the start of the current war.
  • Gap is how many points you need to pass the team above you, and how far ahead you are of the team below.
  • Contributors shows who added the most points.
  • Only the top 2,000 teams get a rank here. Better than % compares a contributor with every player in the top 2,000 leagues (or the top 2,000 clans for clan war). Those numbers come from a full scan of every one of those teams, run every 3 hours. If that scan is missing or more than 2 days old, the page scans in the background instead, with a progress bar and time left, and ranks marked with ~ are estimates until it finishes. Players in the official top players list always get their exact live rank.
For the site owner: share the scan with everyone

Normally the scan on GitHub shares exact numbers with every visitor. If that isn't running, this browser's own scan can stand in: download it when it finishes, name it ranks.json, and upload it next to index.html.

About this Pet Simulator 99 tracker

PS99 Tracker is a free fan-made tool for Pet Simulator 99 (Pet Sim 99, PS99) on Roblox. Look up any league or clan in the top 2,000 to see its rank, points, points per hour, gains over 1, 6, 12 and 24 hours, and the gap to the teams above and below it. Every contributor shows with their Roblox avatar and how they compare to other players in the league war or clan war.

How do I check my league rank in Pet Simulator 99?

Pick League, type your league name, and tap Show my stats. If your league is in the top 2,000 you get its rank, points and gap to the next league.

How do I check my clan rank?

Pick Clan, type your clan name, and tap Show my stats. You also see who contributed the most points and how fast your clan is gaining.

How often does the leaderboard update?

The top 100 and the team you look up refresh every minute, and the full top 2,000 every 5 minutes, from the official BIG Games public API. Points per hour, gains and the chart use snapshots the site saves every hour and keeps for 24 hours.

What does "better than X% of players" mean?

It compares a contributor's points with every player in the top 2,000 leagues (or the top 2,000 clans for clan war). Ranks marked with ~ are estimates while the scan of those teams is still running.

Is this an official tool?

No. It is fan-made and not affiliated with BIG Games or Roblox.
