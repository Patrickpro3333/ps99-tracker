How to read the stats
  • Rank is the place on the leaderboard. #1 is the best.
  • Better than 98% means a player has more points than 98 in 100 players in the top 2,000 leagues, or the top 2,000 clans for the clan war. A ~ means it is an estimate for now.
  • Top 2.6% next to a rank means only 2.6 in 100 leagues or clans are ahead.
  • Points per hour is how fast points are coming in right now.
  • Points gained are the points added in the last 1, 6, 12 and 24 hours.
  • Close rivals shows how much is needed to pass the team above, and how far ahead the team is of the one below.
  • Player finds anyone by Roblox username, as long as their league or clan is in the top 2,000.
  • Clan points start again at zero when a new clan war begins.

About this Pet Simulator 99 tracker

PS99 Tracker is a free fan-made tool for Pet Simulator 99 (Pet Sim 99, PS99) on Roblox. Look up any league or clan in the top 2,000 to see its rank, points, points per hour, gains over 1, 6, 12 and 24 hours, and the gap to the teams above and below it. Every contributor shows with their Roblox avatar and how they compare to other players in the league war or clan war.

How do I check my league rank in Pet Simulator 99?

Pick League, type your league name, and tap Show stats. If your league is in the top 2,000 you get its rank, points and gap to the next league.

How do I check my clan rank?

Pick Clan, type your clan name, and tap Show stats. You also see who contributed the most points and how fast your clan is gaining.

How often does the leaderboard update?

The top 100 and the team you look up refresh every minute, and the full top 2,000 every 5 minutes, from the official BIG Games public API. Points per hour, gains and the chart use snapshots the site saves every hour and keeps for 24 hours.

What does "better than 98%" mean?

The player has more points than 98 in 100 players. It compares a player with every player in the top 2,000 leagues, or the top 2,000 clans for the clan war. A ~ means it is an estimate while the scan of those teams is still running.

How do I look up a player?

Pick Player, type their Roblox username or user ID, and tap Show stats. You see their points, rank and share of points in their league and in the clan war, as long as their league or clan is in the top 2,000.
