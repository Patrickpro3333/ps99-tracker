Pet Simulator 99 clan battle history

Every clan battle in Pet Simulator 99, newest first: when it ran, what each placement won, and the three clans that finished on top. The dates and prizes are the battle settings in BIG Games' public game data, and the placements are each clan's final result.

For the battle that is running now, the live leaderboard shows every clan in the top 2,000 with its points per hour and how far it is from the clans around it.

How clan battles work

A clan battle is a timed event where every clan in the game collects points for one task, such as farming Treats in the Ender Clan Battle or filling the Index in PSX Classic in the Fantasy Clan Battle. Points start at 0 when a battle begins, and the clans with the most points when it ends get the prizes.

  • Every battle starts on a Saturday, between 16:00 and 18:00 UTC. Since December 2024 nearly all of them last 6 days. Most battles in 2024 ran for 13 days, and the first ones up to 20.
  • Since June 2026 a new battle has usually started every two weeks.
  • Prizes depend on the final placement. Since June 2026 the winning clan's top 100 contributors each get a Gargantuan pet, places 2 and 3 a rainbow Titanic, places 4 to 10 a golden Titanic, places 11 to 50 a Titanic and places 51 to 250 a Huge. The top 30 also get the battle's hoverboard, the top 50 its booth, and the top 500 a Clan Gift.
  • From March 2025 to June 2026 only the winning clan got a Titanic: rainbow for its top 3 contributors, golden for the next 7 and a plain one for the rest. Places 2 to 20, and later 2 to 50, got a Huge.
  • From May 2024 to March 2025 the prizes were Huge pets for the top 10 (rainbow for the winner, golden for places 2 and 3), a hoverboard for the top 10, a booth for the top 50 and a Clan Gift for the top 500.
  • The first battles, from December 2023 to May 2024, gave medals instead: Bronze, Silver and Gold, and later also Good, each with its own prize.
  • The list also has two league events from 2026, the Time Trials League and RNG League U79. Clans have no placements in those.

Placements come from each clan's own record, read for the top 2,000 clans of the latest battle and the 1,000 clans with the most diamonds. A clan that was deleted since then can be missing from older battles.
