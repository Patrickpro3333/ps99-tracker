Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

PS99 Tracker (ps99tracker.xyz) is a free, fan-made tracker for Pet Simulator 99. There are no accounts, and the site never asks for your name, email or any other personal details. This page explains what does get stored or shared when you use it.

What your browser keeps

To load faster and remember where you were, the site saves a few things in your browser's local storage: the leagues, clans and players you looked up and your recent searches, a copy of the leaderboards and the current war, and Roblox usernames and user IDs it already looked up. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Services the site talks to

When you look something up, your browser fetches the data from:

  • BIG Games' public Pet Simulator 99 API, for leaderboards, clans and leagues.
  • Roblox, for profile pictures, and for the name of a player the site has no saved copy of.
  • GitHub, where the site keeps its points history and rank data.
  • Google Fonts, for the site's fonts.

Like any website you visit, these services see your IP address and what was requested, which can include the name you searched. Some of these requests go through a small relay on the site's Cloudflare account; Cloudflare keeps a log of those requests for a few days, used only to fix problems. The site doesn't keep your searches anywhere else.

For players in the top 2,000 leagues and clans, the site keeps a copy of their public Roblox username, display name and profile picture address with its rank data on GitHub, so pages can show them. These are the details Roblox shows on every public profile. The copy is refreshed about once a day, and a player who leaves the top 2,000 teams is dropped from it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Ads and cookies

The site shows ads from Google AdSense, which pay for running it. The site's own code sets no cookies; Google's ads and its consent message do.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may also place and read cookies in your browser, use web beacons, and collect your IP address and other identifiers of your device.
  • The address of a page goes to Google along with each ad request. A player page's address holds the player's Roblox ID number, not their username, and the site doesn't ask for an ad while a username is still in the address.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some other vendors' cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google asks for your consent on your first visit before it uses cookies for ads. To change your answer later, clear this site's cookies and the question comes back.

Google explains how it uses this information in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

The site has no sign-up and doesn't ask anyone, including children, for personal information.

Changes

If anything here changes, this page and the date at the top are updated.
